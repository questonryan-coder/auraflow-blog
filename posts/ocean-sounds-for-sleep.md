# Ocean Sounds for Sleep

Ocean waves are a classic sleep sound because they breathe: a slow rise, a break, a wash back out. On a recording, though, that breathing repeats — the same wave, on the same schedule, all night.

AuraFlow ocean sound is generated live, so each wave is built as you hear it. The rhythm stays wave-like; the waves themselves never repeat. Type "ocean" at [auraflowfocus.com](https://auraflowfocus.com) and hear it built live, free to try with no account.

## What you'll hear

Rolling waves at a slow, even pace, with no music and no voices. The pace stays calm and regular in feel while the audio itself keeps changing — the difference between a rhythm and a recording.

## Sleep or focus? Both

At night, waves mask household sound under a slow rhythm many listeners find easy to drift off to. During the day, the same sound works as a steady work backdrop with the Pomodoro timer. More on why the no-loop part matters in [Sleep Sounds That Never Repeat](../sleep-sounds-that-never-repeat/).

## How to play it tonight

1. Press play — no signup.
2. Set the sleep timer, or let it run all night.
3. Mix with rain for a coastal-storm blend, or keep waves alone.

## FAQ

### Do the ocean sounds loop?

No. They are generated live in your browser using the Web Audio API, so there is no file and no loop point, and no wave repeats exactly — whether you listen for 20 minutes or all night.

### Can I play it all night?

Yes. There is no track length to run out, so the sound keeps generating for as long as you leave it playing. If you'd rather it stopped, use the sleep timer to set a stop time.

### Is ocean free?

Yes — an ocean selection is free to try, no account needed. You get one free world to keep, plus a 60-second taste of the others. Pro ($9/month or $79/year, with a 7-day free trial on the monthly plan) unlocks the full library and full sleep modes.

### Can I mix sounds together for sleep?

Yes. Layer sounds — rain over ocean waves makes a coastal-storm blend — and adjust each layer's volume. You can share a mix as a link, and the person who opens it hears the same blend, with no signup required on their end either.
