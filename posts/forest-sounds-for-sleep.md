# Forest Sounds for Sleeping

A night forest is quiet without being silent: wind moving through leaves, the occasional distant sound, nothing sudden. That gentle variation is why many listeners use forest ambience at bedtime — it gives the ear something soft to rest on instead of a silent room.

AuraFlow forest sound is generated live, so the forest never replays the same minute twice. Type "forest" at [auraflowfocus.com](https://auraflowfocus.com) and hear it built live, free to try with no account.

## What you'll hear

Wind and leaf movement as the bed, kept low and even. No sudden animal calls or startling sounds at night levels — this is a sleep mix, not a nature documentary. Layer rain over it for a forest-in-rain blend.

## Sleep or focus? Both

At night, forest ambience masks a quiet house without feeling artificial. During the day, the same sound works as a calm work backdrop with the Pomodoro timer. It runs on the same live engine as everything in [Sleep Sounds That Never Repeat](../sleep-sounds-that-never-repeat/).

## How to play it tonight

1. Press play — no signup.
2. Set the sleep timer, or let it run.
3. Keep it quiet: forest ambience works best just above the room's own silence.

## FAQ

### Does the forest sound loop?

No. The sounds are generated live in your browser using the Web Audio API. There is no recording behind them and no loop point, so the forest doesn't restart or repeat — whether you listen for 20 minutes or all night.

### Can I play it all night?

Yes. There is no track length to run out, so the sound keeps generating for as long as you leave it playing. If you'd rather it stopped, use the sleep timer to set a stop time.

### Is forest free?

Yes — a forest selection is free to try, no account needed. You get one free world to keep, plus a 60-second taste of the others. Pro ($9/month or $79/year, with a 7-day free trial on the monthly plan) unlocks the full library and full sleep modes.

### Can I mix sounds together for sleep?

Yes. Layer sounds — rain over forest is a natural blend, and rain plus brown noise is a common starting mix — and adjust each layer's volume. You can share a mix as a link, and the person who opens it hears the same blend, with no signup required on their end either.
