# Brown Noise for Sleep

Brown noise is a deep, low rumble — closer to a distant waterfall than to static. Many listeners choose it at night because low frequencies are good at masking the sounds that carry through walls: voices, traffic, appliances.

AuraFlow brown noise is generated live in your browser, so the rumble never loops and never restarts. Type "brown noise" at [auraflowfocus.com](https://auraflowfocus.com) and hear it built live, free to try with no account.

## What you'll hear

A continuous, deep, even rumble with no hiss on top and no pattern to learn. It stays the same in character all night while never repeating the same audio — steady, not looped.

## Brown noise vs pink noise vs white noise

White noise is bright and hissy — all frequencies at equal intensity. Pink noise sits in the middle and sounds like steady rainfall. Brown noise emphasises the low end most, which is why many listeners pick it when the goal is masking a noisy house or street. All three are generated live on AuraFlow, and all three are free to compare — the [full noise colors guide](../white-noise-vs-brown-noise-vs-pink-noise/) goes deeper.

### What is the difference between white noise, brown noise, and pink noise?

White noise contains all audible frequencies at equal intensity — a bright, steady hiss. Pink noise tilts toward the lower frequencies, sounding softer, like steady rainfall. Brown noise tilts further still, producing a deep, warm rumble. All three mask sudden background sounds; which one suits you is usually the one you stop noticing fastest, and all three are free to compare.
